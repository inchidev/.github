# inchi on GitHub

Public organization profile for [inchidev](https://github.com/inchidev), matching [inchi.dev](https://inchi.dev).

- `profile/README.md` renders on the organization overview.
- `profile/assets/inchi-cover.png` is the company website’s social card, preserving its wordmark, palette, typography, and four product illustrations.
- `profile/assets/inchi-avatar.png` is the company website’s compact brand mark for the organization avatar.

Brand assets come from `public/og.png` and `public/icon-512.png` in the company website repository. Keep product names, destinations, and contact details aligned with the website. The avatar must be uploaded through GitHub’s organization profile settings.
